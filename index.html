Our Craft

Every holder starts as a block of seasoned mango or sheesham wood. Artisans carve the design by hand, the tealight seat is bored to a standard fit, and each piece is sanded and finished before it is counted into your order.

Bulk Buyer FAQs

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

The MOQ is 50 pieces per design. You can mix finishes (natural / whitewash) within a design, and mixed-design cartons are available on orders of 200+ pieces.

Can I get samples before placing a bulk order?

Yes. Sample pieces are charged at the 50-pc slab rate plus courier. Sample cost is adjusted against your first bulk order of 500+ pieces.

How long does delivery take?

Production and dispatch take 7–10 working days after sample approval and advance. Transit time depends on your city — most metro deliveries land within 3–5 days of dispatch.

Can you make customised holders for us?

Yes — customised tealight holders with your own design or motif can be made to order. The minimum quantity for a customised order is 200 pcs. All orders ship in transparent packing, so we do not offer printed gift boxes or branded packaging.

What are the payment terms for bulk orders?

Standard terms are 50% advance to start production and the balance before dispatch. Terms for repeat and large orders (1,000+ pcs) are discussed on the quote call.

What if some pieces arrive damaged?

Pieces are packed in protective bulk cartons, but if any piece arrives broken, send us a photo within 48 hours of delivery and we replace it in your next dispatch or adjust it in billing.
